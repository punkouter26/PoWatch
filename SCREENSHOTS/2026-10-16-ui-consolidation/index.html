PoWatch UI consolidation: all ten options

Captured 16 October 2026 from the running Development app (guest account, 60 seeded days), at 1366×768 (laptop) and 390×844 (phone). Numbers on the marks are the option numbers below. what was therewhat replaced it

  1. Live duplicates merged. Presence, Object census and Today vs your usual folded into Key metrics and In frame: 13 panels became 10.
  2. One command bar. Range, Ask and JSON download on one row; History's three spread-out buttons became a joined JSON | PDF group plus Share.
  3. Regulars as cards. Responsive cards with sort keys; the merge bar floats up only while something is ticked.
  4. Popups became drawers. The bell opens a side drawer; deleting data and keeping less confirm in a Radzen dialog.
  5. Settings in a drawer. The gear opens Settings over any page (Escape or ✕ closes it); /settings still works as a page.
  6. Live fits the screen. 4×4 desktop grid with two-row Key metrics and In frame and a two-cell Colour & captions; on a phone the stat panels are one swipeable strip.
  7. Readable charts. Hour labels every six hours, short date formats.
  8. One palette and type scale. Deep-blue glass on every page (and a matching light theme), five font sizes, Radzen variables on the same tokens.
  9. History rebalanced. The week sits inside The day; sessions have their own panel on the left; a date picker joins ◀ ▶; each column scrolls on its own.
  10. Shell polish. No more boot-time layout shift; phone section keys moved to a bottom tab bar; every control at least 24 px.
